Defending rights. Defending each other

Welcome to our space for the latest news and actions supporting women human rights defenders around the world. Here you will find up-to-date information on developments, announcements, and public statements, as well as campaigns, events, and initiatives taking place at the local, regional, and international levels. Follow the most recent updates, see what actions are being taken, and stay informed about the efforts that support women defenders and their work.

Be the warmth someone needs

At Warmth, we believe that no one should face hardship alone. Through our donation movement, every contribution, big or small, becomes a lifeline for families and individuals in need. Together, we provide food, shelter, and essential support, so that dignity is restored and hope is never out of reach. Join us and be part of a community where generosity turns into real, lasting change.

Movements

Women human rights defenders have long marched at the forefront of the struggle for justice and equality, from the suffragettes and the Mothers of the Plaza de Mayo to the "Woman, Life, Freedom" movement and #MeToo. They turn streets into spaces of hope and change, while also documenting injustice, supporting those in need, and working to improve laws. Even when the path is difficult, they keep moving forward with courage, showing that lasting change is built through unity and perseverance.

Awards and appreciation

Awards and appreciation: For Women Human Rights Defenders Day 2025, the Dutch Ministry of Foreign Affairs highlighted several defenders nominated for the Human Rights Tulip, including Colombia's Tejido Mujer, represented by Yohana Conda.
